DevOps is a combination of “Development” and “Operations.”
It’s a modern approach that integrates software development (coding, testing, building) with IT operations (deployment, monitoring, maintenance) to deliver applications faster, more reliably, and with higher quality.

Instead of developers and system admins working separately, DevOps promotes collaboration, automation, and continuous feedback throughout the entire lifecycle.
